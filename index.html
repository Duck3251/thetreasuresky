PROLOGUE

The Sky Islands

Higher than ground level, there lays a forgotten land. A group of islands. These islands were known as the SKY ISLANDS. Or not known because they are forgotten. Anyways, the Sky Islands were home to very few creatures. And one of those creatures is SKYDUCK.

CHAPTER 1: SKYDUCK

Skyduck

So on the Sky Islands there lived a duck. That duck thought he was alone on this island. But was he really?

Many readers can have lots of questions about that duck like: What is his name? Who gave birth to him? Is he really alone? And those questions could be answered later on.

But that duck is called Skyduck, and you could have guessed that. But it's okay if you didn't. Even though you are not focused enough to know.

But going back to the story, Skyduck thought he was alone because whenever he looked or went outside, he never saw anyone there. But one day, he went outside. And saw a cow. So he waddled towards it.

CHAPTER 2: SKYCOW

Skycow

Soon after, Skyduck and the cow become somewhat friends. The cow tells Skyduck that his name was Skycow. So the cow is Skycow.

Skyduck follows Skycow into his house. "So come on in, Skyduck. Do you see this?" asked Skycow. Skyduck replied, "These maps?"

There were two maps in front of them. Skycow answered, "Yes. These are my research. I've been researching these maps for a long time now. There have been rumours of treasure on these islands. I've thought that these maps lead to that treasure, but don't know which one."

So now Skycow packed his bag while Skyduck waited. Skyduck and Skycow were going on a journey of a journey of a journey of a journey of a journey of a journey of a journey of a journey of a.......

CHAPTER 3: A JOURNEY LOOPING

A Journey Looping

How would you describe a journey? I don't need your opinion because that was rhetorical.

So Skyduck and Skycow were on their journey for a long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long time.

And in that long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long, long time, a PURPLE SHADOWY MONSTER leaned above Skyduck.

Skyduck was so terrified he could barely talk. He told Skycow, expecting Skycow to help him, but a different outcome happened. The purple shadowy monster was ready to attack, but Skycow didn't see it.

Skyduck closed his eyes, thinking he would be hurt, but instead, he didn't. And when he opened his eyes it was gone. It was gone because it was all his imagination. Skyduck soon noticed that and moved on.

CHAPTER 4: KNOCK KNOCK, WHO KNOCKED?

Knock Knock, Who Knocked?

Soon after... Skyduck and Skycow saw a portal open up. It was a one-way portal, so nothing can go through it from their side.

Skyduck and Skycow were staring at the portal, confused. Soon, some chickens came out. Now Skyduck and Skycow were even more confused.

But it turns out those chickens were EVIL. Soon, Skyduck and Skycow were knocked out.

Luckily, the evil chickens didn't see the maps. So no maps were stolen but...

Is this the end of Skyduck's and Skycow's journey?

CHAPTER 5: UNKNOWN LOCATION

Unknown Location

The Surface is VERY below the Sky Islands, and if anyone went down to the Surface from the Sky Islands, gravity would pull them down, causing them to fall. And falling from that height would take VERY LONG for them to reach the Surface.

Also, it would probably kill them. And the residents of the Surface and the Sky Islands both know that.

So what happened to Skyduck and Skycow? Well, this is where... a cliffhanger resides.
